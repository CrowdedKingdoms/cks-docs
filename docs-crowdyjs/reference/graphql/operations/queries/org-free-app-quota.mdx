:::warning[DEPRECATED]

Free app slots are retired. From allowancePool.effectiveFrom an organization's apps share one free allowance a month; read orgUsageProjection.allowancePools. Before then each app has its own allowance whatever its slot.

:::

An org's free shared-app slot quota and how much of it is used. The slots are a count only: they never change what is free or billed, and apps beyond the quota still publish. Caller must be a member of the org.


```graphql
orgFreeAppQuota(
  orgId: BigInt!
): FreeAppQuota! @deprecated
```
