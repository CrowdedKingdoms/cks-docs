An org's free shared-app slot quota and how much of it is used. Apps beyond the quota still publish; metered usage is billed from the org wallet. Caller must be a member of the org.


```graphql
orgFreeAppQuota(
  orgId: BigInt!
): FreeAppQuota!
```
