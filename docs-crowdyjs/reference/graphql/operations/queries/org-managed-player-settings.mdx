The organization's org-managed player settings: whether it may sign its own players in, its cap, sign-in rate and sign-in lifetimes, how many players it holds, and whether it has accepted the current Org-Managed Players Addendum. Requires manage&#x005F;players on the org (super-admins and operators bypass).


```graphql
orgManagedPlayerSettings(
  orgId: BigInt!
): OrgManagedPlayerSettings!
```
