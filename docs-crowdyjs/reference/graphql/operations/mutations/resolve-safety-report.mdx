Resolve an open report with its outcome: its preserved copy is deleted and the legal hold its reported account got on deletion, if any, is released (a deferred erasure then resumes). Resolving it again changes nothing; an escalated report cannot be resolved (BAD&#x005F;REQUEST). Requires 'moderate&#x005F;safety' on the app (an org role, or the org's admins); super admins bypass. Send the identity session to the app's datacenter.


```graphql
resolveSafetyReport(
  appId: BigInt!
  reportId: BigInt!
  outcome: String!
): SafetyReport!
```
