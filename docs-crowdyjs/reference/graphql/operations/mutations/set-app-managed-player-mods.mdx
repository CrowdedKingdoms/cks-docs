Dev only. Let the organization's org-managed players build and run mods (and use the Studio agent) in this app, or stop them. The organization pays for what they use: their mod compute and egress as its own compute, their project storage as the app's, their model tokens from the org wallet. Turning it off pauses their mods within a minute and refuses new builds. Requires manage&#x005F;billing on the app's organization. Returns the new setting.

setAppManagedPlayerMods(
  appId: BigInt!
  enabled: Boolean!
): Boolean!
