OPERATOR ONLY. Block or open a country, or set its minimum age, with the reason on record. Every instance applies it within a minute; players already signed in there are refused at their next refresh.

```graphql
setTerritoryPolicy(
  input: SetTerritoryPolicyInput!
): TerritoryPolicy!
```

### Type

#### [<code style={{ fontWeight: 'normal' }}><b>TerritoryPolicy</b></code>](/reference/graphql/types/objects/territory-policy.mdx) <Badge class="badge badge--secondary " text="object"/> 
A country Crowded Kingdoms lists: 'blocked' refuses its players (TERRITORY&#x005F;NOT&#x005F;SUPPORTED), and minAge raises the youngest age admitted there. A country not listed is open at the platform's minimum.
