Apps the authenticated caller can see in their account: those owned by an org they are an active member of, OR those where they hold an active app&#x005F;user&#x005F;access grant. Requires authentication. Includes apps of any visibility/status (e.g. drafts the caller can access). Ordered newest-first.


```graphql
myApps: [App!]!
```


### Type

#### [<code style={{ fontWeight: 'normal' }}><b>App</b></code>](/reference/graphql/types/objects/app.mdx) <Badge class="badge badge--secondary " text="object"/> 
A publishable application (game/experience) owned by an organization. Its discoverability is controlled by visibility and its lifecycle by status.
