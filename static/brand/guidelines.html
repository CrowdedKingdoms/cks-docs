Print Rule

Every asset must survive in black and white first. Ember may be printed as a flat spot-like accent when available, but never as a gradient, metallic effect, glow, or dependent contrast cue. If color drops out, hierarchy must still work.

09 — Do & Don'tChecklist

Guardrails,
not restrictions.

A fast reference for anyone shipping a new asset under the Crowded Kingdoms name.

Do

  • Use white space aggressively
  • Let typography do most of the work
  • Keep ember rare and intentional
  • Default to black‑on‑white
  • Use ember for ignition, action, and major signal
  • Build every surface from the same system

Don't

  • Reach for fantasy RPG styling
  • Overuse gold gradients or metallic effects
  • Stack more than two font weights per section
  • Make every section dark
  • Add clutter to communicate "crowded"
  • Use long paragraphs in hero moments
10 — Design Review ChecklistQA

Before you
ship it.

Run any new asset — page, deck slide, social post, UI screen — against this list.

✓Feels bold, minimal, premium, technically powerful
✓Uses black, white, and space as the primary identity
✓Ember appears rarely, and it means ignition
✓Ember is the only bold color
✓Communicates scale without becoming cluttered
✓No crowns, castles, dragons, swords, or parchment
✓Has a clear hierarchy, one primary message
✓Credible to developers, exciting to players
✓Looks like part of a repeatable system
✓Could not be mistaken for a generic SaaS template
