OPERATOR/DESTRUCTIVE: deletes another account and erases it, on Crowded Kingdoms' own word (a parent's request for a child's account, say): exactly what deleteMyAccount does to it, holds included. An open or escalated safety report about the account holds it first, and under a legal hold the account still closes but its content and identity are kept until the hold is released. The erasure record names you and your reason (AccountErasure.requestedBy is `operator:<your id>: <reason>`), and accountErasure(userId) follows it. Refused for your own account, a staff account (operator or super admin) and an account already deleted (requestAccountErasure runs a deleted account's erasure again); NOT&#x005F;FOUND for an unknown user. Send the identity session; any datacenter answers.


```graphql
eraseAccount(
  userId: BigInt!
  reason: String!
): AccountErasure!
```
