Dev only. Register the PUBLIC half of a key your organization signs its players' sign-in assertions with (exchangeManagedPlayerAssertion): its kid, algorithm (EdDSA, ES256 or RS256), the JWK, the iss your assertions carry and, if your identity provider sets its own, their aud. The private key never leaves your systems. Requires manage&#x005F;players on the org.


```graphql
registerOrgSigningKey(
  input: RegisterOrgSigningKeyInput!
): OrgSigningKey!
```
