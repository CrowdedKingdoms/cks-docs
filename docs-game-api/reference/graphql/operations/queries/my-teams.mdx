The caller's teams in an app, with their roles and effective team permissions. Use this to discover which teams the current user belongs to and what they may do in each.


```graphql
myTeams(
  appId: BigInt!
): [GroupMembership!]!
```

The app (tenant) to list the caller's teams within.




### Type

#### [<code style={{ fontWeight: 'normal' }}><b>GroupMembership</b></code>](/reference/graphql/types/objects/group-membership.mdx) <Badge class="badge badge--secondary " text="object"/>
