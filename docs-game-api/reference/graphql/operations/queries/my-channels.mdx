The caller's channels in an app, with their roles and effective channel permissions (e.g. whether they hold send&#x005F;messages). Use this to discover which channels the current user can read/post in.


```graphql
myChannels(
  appId: BigInt!
): [GroupMembership!]!
```


### Arguments

#### [<code style={{ fontWeight: 'normal' }}>myChannels.<b>appId</b></code>](#app-id)<Bullet />[<code style={{ fontWeight: 'normal' }}><b>BigInt!</b></code>](/reference/graphql/types/scalars/big-int.mdx) <Badge class="badge badge--secondary badge--non_null" text="non-null"/> <Badge class="badge badge--secondary " text="scalar"/> \{#app-id\}
The app (tenant) to list the caller's channels within.




### Type

#### [<code style={{ fontWeight: 'normal' }}><b>GroupMembership</b></code>](/reference/graphql/types/objects/group-membership.mdx) <Badge class="badge badge--secondary " text="object"/>
The caller's view of a group they belong to: the group, their roles, and their effective group permission keys.
